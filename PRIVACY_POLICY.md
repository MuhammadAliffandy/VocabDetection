# Privacy Policy for Vocab

**Effective Date:** August 19, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the "Vocab" application ("App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Camera Access
Vocab requires access to your device's camera to capture images of your surroundings. This is the core functionality of the App, allowing it to detect objects and generate vocabulary based on what you see. 
**Crucially, all image processing and object detection are performed entirely on-device.** We do not transmit, upload, or store your photos on any external servers.

### b. Personal Information
We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). You are not required to create an account, provide an email address, or log in to use Vocab.

### c. Usage Data and Analytics
Vocab does not track your individual usage behavior, nor do we use third-party analytics SDKs that collect your data. 

## 2. How We Process Data (On-Device AI)

Vocab prides itself on privacy-by-design. We utilize advanced Machine Learning (CoreML) models that are downloaded to your device via On-Demand Resources (ODR). 
- **Object Detection:** When you capture a photo, the AI model processes the image locally on your iPhone's hardware.
- **Translation:** Vocabulary translation relies on Apple's native translation frameworks. Depending on your iOS settings, this may utilize Apple's servers or run locally if you have downloaded the relevant language packs. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy regarding their translation services.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Vocab relies on Apple's infrastructure (App Store and On-Demand Resources) to deliver the application and its AI models to your device. We do not integrate with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Vocab is designed to be safe for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal data, we inadvertently comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations globally.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
